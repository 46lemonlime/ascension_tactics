ASCENSION TACTICS RULES

Mechanics: Turn-based continuous physical combat inspired by Warhammer 40,000 10th Edition and StarCraft 2 collision steering.

Morale: Taking casualties requires Battleshock tests (2D6 vs Leadership). Broken units suffer severe penalties and must rally.

Line of Sight & Fog of War: Terrain blocks vision. Enemies in Fog of War are hidden from sensors and the action camera.

Controls: Left Click to select/move/attack. Right Click in deployment to undeploy. Drag to reposition units. Ctrl+Z to undo camera.
