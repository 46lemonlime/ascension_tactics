HOW TO PLAY & WARZONE ARCHIVES

Master battlefield movement, line of sight combat, tactical objectives, and study all 8 warhost factions.

🛡️ 1. DEPLOYMENT PHASE

Before battle begins, assemble your strike force in the Southern Sector (Rows 48–55).

  • Placing Squads: Click any unit card in the bottom dock, then click a valid deployment tile. Selection auto-advances to the next unplaced squad.
  • Repositioning & Recall: Click a placed unit on the board to select it. Right-click a placed unit or dock card to recall it.
  • Auto-Deploy: Click AUTO-DEPLOY to instantly position your force into standard tactical formation.
  • Commence Battle: Once all squads are deployed, click COMMENCE BATTLE.
⚡ 2. MOVEMENT & PATHFINDING

Positioning and zone control are critical to victory.

  • Reachable Grid: Left-clicking a player unit highlights all valid movement tiles in Cyan based on the squad's Movement stat (M).
  • Fluid Path Traversal: Left-clicking any highlighted cyan tile smoothly moves the entire squad along the shortest path around obstacles and friendly units.
  • Squad Cohesion: Multi-model squads automatically maintain tactical formation (wedge, line, or echelon) during movement.
💥 3. BALLISTIC & MELEE COMBAT

Engage enemy warbands with heavy weapons or brutal close assault.

  • Targeting: Hostile units within weapon Range (R) and direct Line of Sight are highlighted in Red.
  • Executing Attacks: Click any red hostile target to open fire. Ballistic Skill (BS), Weapon Strength (S), and Damage (D) determine casualties against enemy Toughness (T) and Armor Saves (Sv+).
  • Action Sequence: Each squad may move once and fire once per turn. Moving after firing or shooting on the run depends on weapon classification.
👁️ 4. LINE OF SIGHT & FOG OF WAR

Dynamic line-of-sight simulates true tabletop battlefield vision.

  • Obstacle Occlusion: Solid ruins, ancient temples, dense trees, fossil dunes, and reactor cores block sight lines.
  • Fog of War Shroud: Areas outside your squads' Awareness radius remain hidden in dark tactical fog. Enemy squads in FoW cannot be targeted until spotted.
  • Radar Minimap: The tactical minimap in the upper left renders terrain, detected hostiles, active objectives, and the camera reticle in real time.
💀 5. MORALE & RALLIES

Heavy casualties can break a squad's nerve.

  • Morale Checks: When a unit suffers 50%+ casualties in a turn, it must roll against its Leadership (Ld).
  • Broken Status: Broken units break formation, lose movement effectiveness, and cannot fire until rallied.
  • Rallying: Squads attempt to rally at the start of each round or via Hero Captain aura abilities.
👑 6. MISSIONS & OBJECTIVES

Choose from 3 competitive battlefield missions:

  • Extermination: Total annihilation of all hostile squads.
  • Domination: Control the 3 central tactical nodes (Nodes 1, 2, 3) at the end of each round to accumulate Victory Points (VP). Highest score after 8 rounds or first to 100 VP wins.
  • VIP Escort: Escort the Sacred Relic Courier to the extraction landing zone in the opposing sector, or eliminate the enemy courier.
