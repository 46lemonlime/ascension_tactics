# ⚔️ GRIMDARK TACTICS 3D — 4 Playable Factions Edition

A 3D browser-based **Warhammer 40,000** turn-based squad skirmish game built with **Three.js**, featuring **4 Playable Factions**, an interactive **Race Selection Screen**, tactical **Deployment Phase**, unique procedural 3D miniatures on touching circular bases, and behind-the-attacker action camera.

---

## 🛡️ Playable Factions (Races)

1. **Space Marines (Adeptus Astartes)**:
   - **Colors**: Cobalt Blue & Gold Trim
   - **Playstyle**: Elite power armor (`3+` save), versatile 18" bolters, rapid jump packs, and 26" heavy weapons.
   - **Roster**: *Castellan Vorn (Hero)*, *Tactical Squad Alpha*, *Tactical Squad Beta*, *Assault Squad*, *Devastator Squad*.

2. **Chaos Space Marines (Heretic Astartes)**:
   - **Colors**: Crimson Red, Brass & Daemon Horns
   - **Playstyle**: Brutal melee zealots, daemon axes, corrupted 18" bolters, raptor jump units, and cultist swarms.
   - **Roster**: *Lord Malakar (Hero)*, *Chaos Legionaries Alpha*, *Chaos Legionaries Beta*, *Raptor Squad*, *Cultist Mob*.

3. **Tyranids (Hive Fleet Leviathan)**:
   - **Colors**: Hive Purple Carapace & Bone White Limbs
   - **Playstyle**: Bio-plasma artillery, 20" venom cannons, swift scything talons, rending claws, and 5-man termagant swarms.
   - **Roster**: *Hive Tyrant (Hero)*, *Warrior Brood Alpha*, *Warrior Brood Beta*, *Genestealer Pack*, *Termagant Swarm*.

4. **T'au Empire (Hunter Cadre)**:
   - **Colors**: Ochre Sand & Plasma Cyan Trim
   - **Playstyle**: Technological superiority with extreme-range 24" pulse rifles, 28" hyper-velocity rail rifles, and crisis battlesuits.
   - **Roster**: *Commander Shas'O (Hero)*, *Fire Warriors Alpha*, *Fire Warriors Beta*, *Stealth Battlesuits*, *Pathfinder Snipers*.

## 🎮 Match Flow & Controls

1. **Race Selection Screen**:
   - Choose your faction (*Space Marines, Chaos, Tyranids, or T'au*).
   - Select your AI opponent (or choose `🎲 Random Rival Faction`).
   - Click `COMMENCE DEPLOYMENT &rarr;`.

2. **Deployment Phase**:
   - Select a squad card from the bottom dock.
   - Click any tile in the southern deployment zone (Rows 24–27) to place the squad (or click `🎲 Auto-Deploy`).
   - Click `⚔️ Commence Battle` once all 5 squads are placed.

3. **Combat Phase**:
   - **Left Click**: Select squad &bull; Click blue tile to Move &bull; Click red enemy to Shoot / Melee.
   - **Mouse Hover**: 3D targeting laser and line-of-sight analysis.
   - **Over-the-Shoulder Action Cam**: Swoops behind the attacker looking directly down-range at the target during attacks.
   - **Controls**: Left Click Drag: Orbit &bull; Right Click Drag: Pan &bull; Wheel: Zoom.
